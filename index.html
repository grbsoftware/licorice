A fresh coloring page, every time.

Welcome to Licorice! Each page is a brand-new drawing: soft blobs laid over a hand-drawn grid, cut into dozens of little fields for you to color in. Tap to fill, add lines and shapes, then finish them with glitter, pearl and metallic inks. No two pages are ever the same.

Start coloring Free · runs in your browser · nothing to install

What you can do

Everything works with a finger, a pen or a mouse.

  • Fill

    Tap any field to fill it, or let Auto color do the whole page in rings or at random.

  • Draw

    Add lines and curves on top. Turn on wall and they become part of the outline.

  • Decorate

    Press inside a field to add dots, drops, hearts, stars, spirals and fans, as many as you like.

  • Shimmer

    Finish shape lines in glitter, three-tone pearl, gold, silver, copper, bronze or bismuth.

  • Your way

    Color on light or dark paper, pick palettes, mix your own colors and choose the line color.

  • Keep it

    Save your picture, or keep drawings in your browser and pick up where you left off.
